Our wedding scripture

“A threefold cord is not quickly broken.”

9Two are better than one; because they have a good reward for their labour. 10For if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow: but woe to him that is alone when he falleth; for he hath not another to help him up. 11Again, if two lie together, then they have heat: but how can one be warm alone? 12And if one prevail against him, two shall withstand him; and a threefold cord is not quickly broken.

Ecclesiastes 4:9–12 KJV

A reflection

Two hearts, joined as one, and woven together with God at the centre. On our own we are a single strand; together we lift each other up, keep each other warm and stand firm. With Christ as the third strand, the cord holds — through every season, for a lifetime.

How it began

Our Story

Two people, one faith, and a love that has grown into a lifetime promise. We are grateful to God for bringing us together, and to everyone who has walked this road with us. On 19 December, we begin a new chapter, and we would love you to be there.

— Francisca & Lawrence

Colour inspiration

Brown & Gold

Our wedding colours are rich brown and warm gold. If you’d love to join in, here is a little inspiration — but please wear whatever makes you feel wonderful. This is not a dress code.

For the ladies

Gele & Aso-Oke

  • Champagne or antique-gold aso-oke with a sculpted gele
  • Chocolate-brown lace with gold beading or embroidery
  • Soft neutrals — cream, ivory or nude — with gold accessories

For the gentlemen

Agbada & Tailoring

  • Agbada or kaftan in espresso, chocolate or champagne
